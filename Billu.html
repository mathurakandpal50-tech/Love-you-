Aapka thoda sa pagal... ❤️
01
Yaad hai?

Pichle saal ki woh raaten...

Jab hamare half-yearly papers chal rahe the aur pata hi nahi chalta tha ki raat kab ho gayi. Padhai ke beech mein bhi hamari baatein khatam nahi hoti thi. Ek topic se doosra topic, phir koi random baat, phir hasi-mazaak... aur dekhte-dekhte ghanton nikal jaate the.

Shayad wahi woh time tha jab ham dono ek dusre ke sabse zyada close aaye the. Dheere-dheere ek aisa comfort ban gaya tha jahan kuch bhi batane se pehle sochna nahi padta tha.

02

Woh ajeeb sa comfort...

“Phone dono ke paas tha, phir bhi copy mein likhkar baat karna aur uski photo kheench kar ek dusre ko bhejna...”

Aaj sochta hoon toh woh kitni chhoti si cheez thi, lekin mere liye unhi chhoti cheezon mein toh hamari sabse pyari memories chhupi hain. Hamara baat karne ka woh stupid sa tareeka bhi kitna apna lagta tha.

03
Aur phir...

Meri jealousy 😂

Kabhi school mein aap kisi aur ladke se baat kar leti thi aur mera mood kharab. Main chid jaata tha, aapko pata hota tha ki main kyun chidh raha hoon... aur phir aap bhi mujhe aur chidhane lagti thi.

Aur jab main sach mein gussa ho jaata tha, tab aapka woh cute sa drama karke manana... Yrr 😭❤️ uss waqt gussa bhi aata tha, par andar se hasi bhi aa rahi hoti thi.

04

Aap meri baat ko...

Kabhi-kabhi mujhe lagta tha ki main jo bhi bol raha hoon, aap usse unnecessarily serious le rahi ho. Lekin phir samajh aaya ki aap meri baaton ko importance deti thi. Meri chhoti si baat ko bhi apni priority bana leti thi.

Aur haan, main ye bhi nahi bhool sakta ki main jis cheez ko mana karta tha, aap kabhi-kabhi wahi karke mujhe aur chidhati thi. 😂 Shayad aapko meri reaction dekhna zyada pasand tha.

05
The best part

School se ghar aate hi...

Aap apni problems lekar mere paas aa jaati thi. Jo school mein hua, kis baat ka mood kharab hai, kis cheez ne pareshaan kiya... sab kuch.

Aur pata hai? Mujhe woh bahut special lagta tha. Isliye nahi ki aapki problems solve karna meri responsibility thi, balki isliye kyunki aapko mere saamne apna real self dikhane mein comfort tha. Aapko lagta tha ki aap bina filter ke mujhe sab bata sakti hain.

06

“Sorry” × 1000 😭

Aap gussa hoti thi toh meri taraf se bas ek hi mission hota tha— aapko manana. Kabhi ek sorry, kabhi dus sorry, aur kabhi toh literally itne sorry ki khud mujhe yaad nahi rehta tha kitni baar likh diya.

Aur jab words kaam na karein, toh gaana gaa kar manana... kyunki meri Billu ko manana bhi koi normal kaam thodi hai. 😭❤️

Dil se

Yrr Billu...

Maaf kar do na. Sach mein galti ho gayi mujhse. Main ye nahi keh raha ki maine kabhi jaan-bujhkar aapko hurt nahi kiya. Shayad meri kuch baatein, mera gussa, meri immaturity... kuch na kuch aisa raha hoga jiski wajah se aapko bura laga.

Bas itna chahta hoon ki jo kuch bhi galat hua, uski wajah se hamari woh purani hasi, woh random conversations, woh comfort aur woh pagalpan completely khatam na ho.

07

Main aapke saath...

Kabhi aapki aankhon mein aansu dekhna nahi chahta. Main chahta hoon ki jab life mein koi problem aaye, toh aapko lage ki aapko sab kuch akela handle nahi karna hai. Main har problem solve kar paunga, aisa promise nahi karunga, lekin aapke saath khada rehne ki poori koshish zaroor karunga.

Aur jab aapke life mein koi khushi aaye, toh main us khushi ko aur bada karne wala insaan banna chahta hoon.

08
Maybe thoda foolish hai...

Par aapke saath...

“Main bhi thoda bachcha ban jaata hoon.”

Pata nahi kyun, par aapke saath mujhe har waqt mature banne ki acting nahi karni padti. Kabhi stupid baatein, kabhi bina matlab ke hasi, kabhi chhoti si baat par chidna... bas jo mann mein hota hai woh bol deta hoon.

Aur shayad isi liye dil karta hai ki aapke saare dreams poore hote hue dekhu aur jitna mere bas mein ho, utna un dreams ke saath chalun.

09

Kuch memories abhi baaki hain...

Main chahta hoon ki hamari story sirf purani memories tak limited na rahe. Abhi toh bahut kuch dekhna baaki hai.

Ek bike trip...
Raaste mein mountains...
Khoobsurat nazare...
Kahin ruk kar Maggie...
Aur bas kuch der duniya se door...

Shayad ye sab sunne mein thoda filmy lage, par mujhe sach mein aapke saath aise moments imagine karna achha lagta hai.

10
Ek aur sach

Kabhi main akela feel karun...

Toh sabse pehle mann kare ki aapko bataun. Apni problems, apna stress, apni khushi, koi stupid si baat jo kisi aur ko interesting bhi na lage... bas aapko bata doon.

Kyunki mere liye comfort ka matlab sirf achhe time mein saath rehna nahi hai. Comfort ka matlab hai ki bure time mein bhi bina darr ke keh paana— “Yrr, aaj kuch theek nahi lag raha.”

Before the question...

Billu, ek cheez samajh aayi...

Ham perfect nahi hain. Hamari fights hui hain, misunderstandings hui hain, gussa hua hai, kabhi maine galat kiya, kabhi shayad aapko meri koi baat samajh nahi aayi.

Lekin itni saari cheezon ke beech bhi, mere dil mein aapke liye jo care hai, woh kabhi fake nahi thi.

Isliye aaj main bade-bade promises nahi kar raha. Bas ek chance maang raha hoon ki jo achha tha, usse aur achha banane ki koshish karein.
